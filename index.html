About me

I work on multi-agent systems and safe AI, and I am seeking PhD positions for Fall 2027.

My background is in catching failures before they spread. At Texas Instruments, I worked as a Design Verification Engineer, using formal and simulation-based verification to find bugs in chips before they shipped. Before that, I did hardware security research in the FICS Lab at the University of Florida, protecting neural-network inference on FPGAs from side-channel leakage and hardening IC metering protocols against fault-injection attacks.

I now want to bring that mindset to teams of learning agents: noticing when one agent starts to fail, forecasting how the error will spread, and building systems that recover before it becomes a team failure.

Research interests

I’m interested in multi-agent systems: how agents learn, coordinate, and fail, and how to make them robust, reliable, and safe.

Multi‑Agent Learning & Coordination

How teams of learning agents cooperate, compete, and coordinate under partial information, in multi-agent RL and LLM agent systems.

Fault Detection & Forecasting

Detecting errors early and forecasting how they spread through a team of agents, so systems can recover before one agent’s mistake becomes a team failure.

Safe & Secure Agent Systems

Keeping agent systems safe and secure, from guarantees in safety-critical settings to agents that act on people’s behalf and keep them in control.
